ABOUT US

Heading - Who We Are

Subheading - A Library For Teenage Girls

Teenagera is Pakistan's first digital library made specially for teenage girls. We believe books can change mood, mindset and future. Our collection is selected to help you grow, feel confident, and learn about life.

Heading - Our Mission

Subheading - Why We Started

We started Teenagera because teenage girls need safe, inspiring and useful books. Not boring library books. Real books about self-growth, friendships, family, dreams, and becoming strong women.

Heading - What Makes Us Different

Subheading - Curated With Love

Every book in Teenagera library is hand-picked. We read reviews, check age suitability, and only add books that are meaningful for 13-19 years girls. No random books.

Heading - Join Teenagera Family

Thousands of girls across Pakistan are part of Teenagera. Follow us on Instagram and WhatsApp for new books.
